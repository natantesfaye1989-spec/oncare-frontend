Common questions

Questions riders and families ask us.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Call or use the form by 5 PM for a next-day ride. For treatment that repeats — dialysis, therapy — set a standing order once, and every ride in the series is scheduled automatically.

Can I book for my mother, or for a client?

Yes. The request form asks who is filling it in — rider, family member, facility scheduler or broker. We plan the details with you by phone, and the rider decides who receives trip updates.

Will the driver help me to the door?

Always. Your driver parks, comes to the door, and helps with coats, belts, walkers and steps. At the clinic you are walked in and signed in at the desk. Door through door is the standard, not an upgrade.

Can I ride in my own wheelchair?

Yes. Our vans carry a lift and four-point tie-downs, and every driver is trained in securement. Mention the chair on the booking call and the right van is assigned automatically.

How do I know the ride went okay?

With the rider’s agreement, up to three people get plain text messages at every step: on the way, on board, and signed in at the desk. No app to install — and the rider can change the list or stop the texts at any time.

What does a ride cost?

Many riders pay nothing: Medicaid often covers the trip through your broker — call [Broker name] at [(555) 000-0000] and ask for OnCare Transit by name.

Paying privately? We quote the full price on the phone before you commit, and it appears in writing with your confirmation.
